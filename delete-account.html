Delete Your Account

How to permanently delete your TallUp account and all associated data.

Deletion is permanent. Your account, email, and all cloud-backed-up progress (profile, workout, height, sleep, and nutrition data) are erased and cannot be recovered.

Delete in the app (recommended, immediate)

  1. Open TallUp on your device.
  2. Tap the gear icon (top right) to open Settings.
  3. Make sure you are signed in — your email appears in the account card at the top.
  4. Tap Delete account below the account card.
  5. Read the confirmation and tap again to confirm permanent deletion.

Your account and cloud data are deleted immediately, local progress on the device is reset, and you are signed out.

Delete by email request (fallback)

If you no longer have access to the app or your device, email us from the address linked to your account:

  1. Send an email to privacy@tallup.app with the subject "Delete my TallUp account".
  2. We will verify the request came from the account's email address and confirm once deletion is complete (normally within 7 days).

What gets deleted

  1. Your authentication account (email + credentials).
  2. Your cloud-stored progress snapshot (profile, completions, height/sleep/food logs, settings).
  3. Local on-device data — reset automatically when you delete in-app; otherwise erased by uninstalling the app or via Settings → Reset profile & progress.

An active Premium subscription is billed by Apple or Google — deleting your TallUp account does not cancel it. Cancel first in your store account settings (iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions; Android: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions). Store transaction records are retained by Apple/Google as required for receipts and refunds.

No account?

TallUp works without an account and stores everything only on your device. In that case there is nothing on our servers — just use Settings → Reset profile & progress or uninstall the app.
